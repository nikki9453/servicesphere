About Service Sphere

A hyper-local service discovery and appointment prototype tailored specifically for Unnao, Uttar Pradesh.

BCA Minor Project Frontend Prototype

"Find the right service, right when you need it."

Service Sphere is a local service discovery and booking prototype built to address the unique needs of residents in Unnao, Uttar Pradesh. While large metro-centric platforms focus on mega-cities, towns like Unnao often rely on informal word-of-mouth or unverified phone directories to find skilled tradespeople.

This project provides a clean, standardized, and accessible digital platform where Unnao residents can:

  • Discover local services: Browse 8 everyday essential categories including electricians, plumbers, house cleaners, and repair technicians.
  • Compare local providers: Inspect public business details, specializations, and service coverage across Civil Lines, Lok Nagar, Narendra Nagar, and other Unnao sectors.
  • View genuine directory profiles: Review transparent information without fabricated ratings, artificial awards, or inflated metrics.
  • Request and book services: Schedule appointment visits with structured dates, preferred time windows, and local addresses.
  • Manage booking lifecycles: Track request confirmations, cancellations, and service records through localStorage-backed state management.
🛡️
Data Authenticity Commitment: In accordance with academic honesty and design ethics, this platform does not fabricate customer reviews, awards, or fake subscriber numbers. Unverified fields clearly display "Information not available" or "Contact provider for pricing".

How It Works

1
Choose a Category

Browse from essential household and repair trades active throughout Unnao.

2
Compare Providers

Filter by specific Unnao localities (e.g. Narendra Nagar, Civil Lines, Gadan Khera) and read transparent service capabilities.

3
Schedule Doorstep Visit

Submit an appointment booking with your address and receive a unique Unnao booking reference ID.

4
Provider Dispatch & Review

Providers manage incoming jobs via the dedicated portal, and residents can leave honest community feedback upon job completion.

Technical Specifications

Built strictly in accordance with BCA project requirements using zero heavy frameworks or bloated dependencies:

HTML5 & Semantic Structure Standards-compliant landmarks, accessible forms, and modular templates.
Pure CSS3 (Dual Themes) Custom CSS variables for Sunlit Day and After Hours Night palettes, 3-column CSS Grid layout.
Vanilla JavaScript Client-side routing simulation, dynamic DOM rendering, real-time filtering, and sort logic.
Web Storage (localStorage) Persistent state for dual themes, demo authentication, bookings, reviews, and provider records.
